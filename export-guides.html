Export guides

Two files, about ten minutes.

Your Money Audit needs your invoices and your incoming payments as CSV files. Here is exactly how to get each one. Menus shift a little between software versions, but the words stay close to these.

Invoices from QuickBooks Online

  1. Sign in at qbo.intuit.com.
  2. In the left menu choose Sales (in some versions, Get paid & pay), then Invoices.
  3. Set the date filter to All dates, or at least the last 12 months. The audit is only as complete as the export.
  4. Find the export icon above the list (a box with an arrow, or the printer icon next to it) and choose Export to Excel.
  5. Open the downloaded file and save it as CSV: File, Save As, format CSV. Done.

Any invoice list export works as long as it includes the invoice number, customer, dates, amount, and balance. Extra columns are fine; we ignore what we do not need.

Payments from Stripe

  1. Sign in at dashboard.stripe.com.
  2. Choose Payments in the left menu.
  3. Click Export at the top right.
  4. Set the date range to the last 12 months, keep the default columns, and click Export. A CSV downloads.

Payments from your bank

  1. Sign in to your business bank account.
  2. Open your checking account and look for Download, Export, or Statements & documents.
  3. Choose CSV (sometimes labeled "Excel" or "spreadsheet"), set the range to the last 12 months, and download.

If your bank caps exports at 90 days per file, download several and send them all. We stitch them together.

Anything else with a CSV button

Square, PayPal, Wave, FreshBooks, Xero, HoneyBook: they all export CSVs from their invoice and payment screens. If it has the invoice number or the payer name, a date, and an amount, we can work with it. Not sure? Email the file to support@owneraudits.com and we will tell you straight.
