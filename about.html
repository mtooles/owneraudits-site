About

Built by an operator who wouldn't hand her data to anyone. Including us.

OwnerAudits was not built by a software company. It was built by a career operator: a CEO and CFO in the online industry who has run the money side of multiple companies, including a multi-million-dollar online business she runs today. Her work is the unglamorous machinery that keeps a business alive: collections, reconciliation, lead flow, payroll, and knowing the numbers cold.

Why this exists

When AI tools arrived, they all wanted the same thing from her companies: connect your accounts, upload your customer lists, let your financial data live on their servers, and pay every month for the privilege. She was never comfortable with that. A business's invoices, inbox, and customer list do not belong inside other companies' platforms, and she wasn't willing to put hers there.

So she built the alternative for her own companies first: a small workforce of AI agents that runs entirely on the computer it is installed on. It reconciles the invoices against the deposits, scores the leads, reads the inbox for money sitting unanswered, checks the marketing against real industry benchmarks, and writes an owner's report against her goals every month. Nothing uploaded. Nothing subscribed to. Nobody else's servers.

That is the product you are looking at. Not a demo built to raise money. The productized version of a system a working CFO built because she needed it, ran on real companies, and hardened through real month-ends.

The advent of AI changed what you should pay for

Subscriptions made sense when software had to live in the cloud to be smart. That era is over. With today's AI, the analysis a business used to rent for $200 or more per month, or buy from a consultant for thousands of dollars, can run locally on an ordinary computer, against your own files, with your own keys. When the software runs on your machine, two things become true at once: your data is actually safe, because it never travels, and there is nothing for anyone to charge you monthly for. You should own it.

That is why OwnerAudits is sold the way it is: $499, one time. You download the system, connect your own tools, and it is yours. It audits your business this month and every month after, and we never see a single row of your data. There is no account with us. There is no server of ours with your numbers on it. That is not a policy promise that depends on us behaving; it is how the product is built.

How we keep it honest

We sell analysis and tools, not outcomes; what you do with the findings is what moves your numbers. Every industry comparison the system makes is cited to a published source and year, and if it does not have a benchmark it says so instead of inventing one. And the guarantee is plain: if your first audit surfaces nothing you can act on, email us within 14 days and you get a full refund.
