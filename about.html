About

We sell the audit and the tool. Not a subscription.

Subscriptions are how software companies get paid every month for work they did once. We think an audit should work the other way around: we do the work once, and you keep the tool forever.

OwnerAudits exists because small businesses leak money in the same three places, unpaid invoices, unanswered email, and unmeasured leads, and because the fix is rarely more effort. It is usually just visibility, delivered once, and a system that keeps the visibility fresh. That is what each audit is: the report that shows you exactly where you stand today, and the agent that re-runs the same analysis on your machine any time you want it again.

The audits are not theory. Each one is a productized version of a system we built for ourselves and run daily inside our own companies: the same reconciliation, the same inbox triage, the same source scoring, hardened by real use before it was ever offered for sale.

How we keep it honest

Every audit carries the same guarantee: if it surfaces nothing you can act on, you get a full refund within 14 days. Your data is used to produce your audit and then deleted. The agent runs on your own computer and your own accounts, so you are never dependent on us being around. And we sell analysis and tools, not outcomes; what you do with the findings is what moves the numbers.
