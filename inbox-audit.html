The report

  • Promises still open in writing: the "I'll send that over Friday" messages that Friday forgot.

The agent

What you keep

  • The Inbox Agent: triage that runs on your own computer against your own mailbox, draft-first, every day if you want it.
  • A weekly open-loops brief: what is unanswered, what is aging, what was promised.

  1. A mailbox export from Gmail or Google Workspace (we send exact click-by-click instructions; it is about 5 minutes of clicking and a wait while Google prepares the file).

Your mail is used only to produce your audit and is deleted after delivery. Nothing is read by a human beyond quality-checking your report.
