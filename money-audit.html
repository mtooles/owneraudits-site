The report

What the audit finds

  • What is collectable right now, ranked by age, with the follow-up emails already written in your voice. You read, you press send.
  • Money your books are lying about: deposits that never got matched to an invoice, and invoices marked open that were actually paid.
  • Customers who quietly went silent: the regulars who stopped buying and nobody decided that on purpose, with the value of their quiet stretch computed.
  • Who your revenue depends on: concentration in your top customers, your recurring-versus-one-off mix, and which frequent clients are retainer-ready.
  • Pricing and scope signals: shrinking invoice sizes, habitual slow payers, and the terms changes that fix them.
  • The Recovery Map: every finding turned into a move, ranked by the size of the money involved, each with its effort stated honestly.
