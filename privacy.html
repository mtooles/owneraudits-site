Privacy Policy

Effective July 25, 2026. OwnerAudits is a product of Woke Publishing Inc.

What we collect

When you buy an audit, we collect your name, email address, and the business data exports you choose to upload (for example invoice lists, payment records, mailbox exports, or lead lists). Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.

How we use it

Your uploaded data is used for exactly one purpose: producing your audit report and configuring your agent. We do not sell your data, share it with third parties for marketing, or use it to train anything.

How long we keep it

Uploaded business data is deleted after your audit is delivered. Your name, email, and order record are kept for accounting and support.

The agent and your data

After delivery, the agent runs entirely on your own computer and your own accounts. We have no access to it, and nothing it processes ever reaches us.

Email

We send transactional email about your order (intake instructions, delivery, setup help). You can opt out of anything beyond that at any time.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (pages viewed, general location, device type). It sets cookies for that purpose. Nothing you upload for an audit and nothing about your order is ever sent to it.

Questions

Write to support@owneraudits.com and a human will answer.
