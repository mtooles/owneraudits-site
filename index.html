The full Business Audit report plus the agent system that produced it, a setup guide, and a walkthrough video. Your agents re-run the audit monthly against your goals, on your computer. Nothing renews.

A packaged AI assistant that re-runs your audit on your own computer, on your own data. It runs on a standard Claude subscription (about $20 per month, paid to Anthropic, not to us). The setup guide assumes no technical background and the video walks every click.
