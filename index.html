Questions

The honest FAQ.

What exactly do I get for $497?

Four things, delivered by email: the written audit report on your business, the packaged agent that produced it, a plain-English setup guide, and a walkthrough video. Nothing ships in a box and nothing renews.

What is "the agent" and what do I need to run it?

A packaged AI assistant that re-runs your audit on your own computer, on your own data. It runs on a standard Claude subscription (about $20 per month, paid to Anthropic, not to us). The setup guide assumes no technical background and the video walks every click.

Is my data safe?

You export your own data from your own tools and upload it through a secure form. We use it for exactly one purpose, producing your audit, then delete it after delivery. We never sell it, share it, or use it to train anything. Full details in the privacy policy.

How fast is delivery?

Upload instructions arrive within a few hours of purchase. Your report and agent arrive within 48 hours of us receiving your complete upload.

What if the audit finds nothing?

Then you should not pay for it. Tell us within 14 days of delivery and we refund you in full.

Is there really no subscription?

Really. You pay once and own everything we send. The agent re-runs as often as you like at no further cost from us. We would rather earn your next purchase than bill you quietly every month.
