FAQ and support

Everything worth asking before you buy.

And after. This page is the support desk: most answers live here, and anything it misses reaches a human at support@owneraudits.com.

The product

What exactly do I get for $499?

The full Business Audit report (health score, identified recoveries, every chapter your data supports) plus the complete agent system that produced it, a plain-English setup guide, and a walkthrough video. Your agents re-run the audit monthly against your goals, on your computer. Nothing ships in a box, nothing renews, and there is no month two.

What is "the agent," concretely?

A packaged AI assistant, a set of instructions and small programs, that re-runs your audit on your own computer against your own fresh data. You run it whenever you want: weekly for receivables, daily for inbox triage, monthly for lead scoring. It works for you, on your machine, and we have no access to it after delivery.

What do I need to run the agent?

A computer and a standard Claude subscription from Anthropic (about $20 per month, paid to Anthropic, not to us). The setup guide assumes no technical background; the walkthrough video shows every click. Typical setup is under 30 minutes.

Do the audits work with my tools?

At launch we support exports from QuickBooks Online, Stripe, Gmail and Google Workspace, plus CSV files from any CRM, form tool, or spreadsheet. If your setup is unusual, email us before buying and we will tell you straight whether it fits.

Data and trust

Is my data safe?

Safer than safe: it never leaves your computer. The workforce runs locally and connects to your tools with your own credentials; we never see a record, a key, or a password. There is nothing to send us and nothing for us to delete. The full policy is on the privacy page.

Who is behind OwnerAudits?

OwnerAudits is a product of Woke Publishing Inc., a Florida company. The audits are productized versions of the systems we run daily inside our own businesses. More on the about page.

Why should I trust an audit at this price?

Because the guarantee makes trust unnecessary: if your audit surfaces nothing you can act on, you tell us within 14 days and we refund all of it. We can offer that because the finding rate on real business data is high, and because we keep our delivery costs low with the same automation we are selling you.

Delivery and support

How fast is delivery?

Your download arrives by email immediately after checkout. Setup is 30 to 45 minutes once, and part of that is a real get-to-know interview: the workforce asks about your business, your model, and your biggest problems before analyzing anything, which is exactly why the audit comes out specific instead of generic. After setup, your first report and dashboard appear minutes after you say "run my business audit."

What does support look like after delivery?

The setup guide and video answer nearly everything. This page collects the rest. If you are stuck after both, email support@owneraudits.com and a human answers. What we do not do is calls, retainers, or monthly check-ins; the product is designed so you never need us again.

What is the refund policy, exactly?

If your audit surfaces nothing you can act on, tell us within 14 days of delivery and we refund your purchase in full to your original payment method. Plain terms on the terms page.

Can the workforce learn to do more later?

Yes. OwnerAudits builds small custom add-on agents for existing customers, typically $59.99, delivered into your same folder: a review-watcher, a subcontractor-paperwork chaser, whatever your business needs. Email support@owneraudits.com with one line about what you want. If you choose to attach your latest report, the new agent arrives already fitted to your business; always your choice.

Is it one audit or several?

One product. Your audit runs every chapter your data supports: money, customers, leads, inbox, and marketing. The more exports you send, the more chapters you get, at the same $499.
